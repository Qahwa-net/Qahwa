# Reports

**Reports** (under My work) lists the reports you can open: your own and those shared with everyone.

## Making a report
Choose **New report**, give it a title and pick the **DocType**. Then choose the kind:

- **A list of documents**: tick the columns to show; add filters ("only documents where Region is North"); choose the order and how many rows (up to 1000).
- **A summary**: choose a field to **group by** and the **numbers** to show for each group: count, total, average, smallest or largest of a numeric field (up to 5).

**Preview** shows the result as you will see it. **Save** keeps the report. Tick **Share with everyone** to let others open it.

## What people see
A report is always run **for the person who opens it**, with their own permissions. If you may not see some documents or fields, the report does not show them to you, and if you may not use reports on that DocType you are told so. Sharing a report never gives anyone extra access.

## Opening and downloading
Open a report to see its table; **Refresh** runs it again and **Download CSV** gives a spreadsheet file (needs permission to export). Only the owner can change or delete a report. If more than the limit of rows match, a note says only the first rows are shown: add a filter.
