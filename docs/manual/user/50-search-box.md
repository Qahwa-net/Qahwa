# The search box

Press **Ctrl+K** (⌘K on a Mac) anywhere, or the **Search** button at the top, and start typing.

- **Pages and lists**: type part of a name ("sup" finds *Supplier*, *Dashboards*…). Words work from the start of any word: "order" finds *Sales Order*. Capital letters and accents do not matter.
- **Create**: type `new` and a name, for example `new customer`, to jump to a blank form. Only DocTypes you may create are offered.
- **Open a list**: `open customer` (or just `customer`).
- **Find a document**: from two letters on, documents whose title (or name, when the DocType has no title) contains your text are listed under their DocType. You only ever see documents you are allowed to see.

Use ↑ and ↓ to choose and **Enter** to go; **Esc** closes the box.

Characters such as `%`, `_` and `[` are searched for as they are. Up to 5 documents per DocType and 30 in all are shown, newest first; you can search up to 120 times a minute.
