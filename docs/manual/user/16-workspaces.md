# Workspaces: your starting page

A **workspace** is a page on the home screen that your administrator arranged for your role: rows of shortcuts to the things you use, headings, and dashboards with live numbers. If you belong to several roles you may see several workspaces as tabs.

You only ever see shortcuts to things you are allowed to use; if a link is missing, you do not have access to what it leads to.

## Making a workspace (System Managers)

1. Open **Workspaces** under Tools and click **New workspace**.
2. Give it a **Title** and a **Position** (smaller numbers come first).
3. Under **Shown to people with these roles** add the roles that should see it. Leave it empty to show it to everyone.
4. Click **Add a block** and choose its type:
   - **Heading**: a line of text to organise the page.
   - **Shortcuts**: a row of links. For each choose what it opens: the documents of a DocType, a dashboard, or one of the Desk's pages (such as Dashboards or Auto Repeat). Give it a label.
   - **Dashboard**: shows the tiles of a dashboard right on the page. Make the dashboard shared with everyone if the whole role should see it.
5. Use the arrows to reorder blocks, the bin to remove one, and **Save workspace**.

Shortcuts can only open DocTypes, dashboards and Desk pages, not outside websites. Headings are shown exactly as typed.
