# Board and calendar

Besides the list, many DocTypes can be shown as a **board** or a **calendar**. The buttons **Board** and **Calendar** appear in the list toolbar when the DocType has a choice field (Select) or a date field.

## Board
- Each column is one choice of a field, for example Open, Working, Done. Choose another field with **Columns from**.
- **Drag a card** to another column to change its value, or use the small select on the card (**Move … to**), which also works with the keyboard and on a phone.
- If the change is not allowed (you may not edit it, or a rule refuses) the card goes back and the reason is shown.
- A column called **No value** holds documents where the field is empty. Values that are no longer choices get their own column so nothing is hidden.
- Without write permission you can look at the board but not move cards. Click a card's title to open it.

## Calendar
- Shows one month. Use the arrows to change month and **Today** to come back. Choose which date with **By**.
- Click a document on a day to open it. A day shows three documents; the rest are counted as "+N more" (open the list filtered by date to see them).

Both views show the 500 most relevant documents and tell you if there are more.
