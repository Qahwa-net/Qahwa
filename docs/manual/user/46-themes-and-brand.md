# Light and dark themes, and your organisation's look

## Choosing light or dark
Press the round button at the top (◐ ☀ ☾) to go round **automatic → light → dark**. You can also choose under **My account → Appearance**:
- **Organisation default**: what your organisation set;
- **Automatic**: light or dark following your device, changing when it does;
- **Light** or **Dark**: always.

Your choice is kept in this browser.

## For a System Manager: the brand
Open **Tools → Brand**:
- **Name**: shown in the header, on the sign-in page and as the page title (empty means Qahwa).
- **Logo**: a PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP or SVG picture up to 100 KB. It is shown in the header and on the sign-in page. SVG pictures must be plain drawings: ones with scripts or links to other addresses are refused.
- **Brand colour** and **colour for the dark theme**: used for the name, the main buttons and highlights. Text on them is chosen to be readable. If you give no dark colour, a lighter version of the brand colour is used on the dark theme so it stays visible.
- **Theme for people who have not chosen one**: Automatic, Light or Dark.
- **Message on the sign-in page**: up to 300 characters of plain text.

The preview at the bottom of the page shows the colours. Press **Save**; everyone sees the new look when a page next loads. The settings are also available as the document **Brand Settings**.
