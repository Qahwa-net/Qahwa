# Email digest

Turn on a digest in **My account → Email digest** to get one email, daily or weekly, with your unread notifications and open assignments. Choose the hour it arrives (in UTC).

- It is sent only to you, at your own email address.
- If there is nothing to tell you, no email is sent.
- Reading the email does not mark anything as read; open the notification or finish the assignment in Qahwa.
- Turn it off with the same checkbox.
