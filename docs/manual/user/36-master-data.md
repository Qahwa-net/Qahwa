# Countries, currencies, languages and other lists

The system comes with ready-made lists that forms can pick from: **Country** (with its currency), **Currency**, **Language**, **Salutation** and **Gender**. They are filled in the first time the system starts. Everyone who is signed in can read them; only a System Manager can add, change or remove entries (for example a currency your country introduced, or a salutation you want).

Fields that refer to them are searchable lists: type part of a name and choose. Nothing you change is overwritten when the system is upgraded.

## The "All" role
Every signed-in person automatically has the role **All**. In **Role Permissions** you can give **All** the right to *read* a DocType when everyone should be able to see it. Be careful: anything you give to **All** is visible to every signed-in person.
