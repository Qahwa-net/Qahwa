# Assigning, following and notifications

## Assigning a document to someone

Open a saved document. Who it is assigned to is shown in the side panel on the right (below the form on a phone). Click the **Assign** icon (a person with a plus) at the top, or the **+** in that panel, to open the Assign dialog.

1. In **Person** type part of a name or email and choose the person.
2. Optionally write **what needs doing**, pick a **due date** and a **priority**.
3. Click **Assign** (or press Enter).

The person gets a notification, the document appears in **Assigned to me** on their home page, and a grey note is added to the document's Activity. If they could not open the document before, they are given permission to **read** it so the link works.

You can assign a document if you could also **share** it (you can edit it through your role). When the work is finished, the assignee (or whoever assigned it) clicks the tick icon (**Mark as done**); use the bin icon (**Remove assignment**) to take it back. Assigning the same person twice does nothing the second time.

## Following

**Follow** (top right of a saved form) means you are told when someone else **changes** the document or **comments** on it. The number shows how many people follow. You follow automatically what you **create**, **comment on** or are **assigned**; click **Following ✓** to stop.

You only hear about documents you are allowed to open.

## Notifications

The **bell** in the top bar shows how many notifications you have not read (it refreshes every half minute). Click it to see them, newest first:

| Type | When |
|---|---|
| Mention | someone wrote `@your.email` in a comment (you see the start of the comment) |
| Assignment | something was assigned to you, or an assignment you made was completed |
| Comment | a document you follow got a comment |
| Update | a document you follow was changed by someone else |
| Share | someone shared a document with you |

Click a notification to open the document (it is marked read), or **Mark all read**. You never get notifications about your own actions, and deleting a document removes the notifications about it.
