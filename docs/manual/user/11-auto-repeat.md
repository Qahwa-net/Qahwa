# Auto Repeat: documents that copy themselves

Some documents are the same every time: the monthly retainer invoice, the weekly stock check, the yearly renewal. **Auto Repeat** makes a fresh **draft** copy of a document on a schedule, so nobody has to remember.

## Setting one up

1. Open the document and click the **Repeat** icon (two arrows in a loop) at the top.
2. Choose **How often**: daily, weekly, monthly, every 3 months or yearly.
3. Choose **First copy on**. It cannot be in the past.
4. Optionally choose **Stop after**: no copies are made after that date.
5. If the document has date fields that should move with each copy (an invoice date, a due date), tick them: each copy gets the date it is made on.
6. Click **Repeat**.

You must be allowed to open the document and to create new ones of its kind.

## What happens

- On the day, a new draft appears. It is **yours** (you own it, you follow it, and you get a notification). Table rows are copied. Anything unique, such as a reference number, is left empty for you to fill in, and calculated fields work out their own values.
- Assignment rules, workflows and naming series apply to the copy as to any new document. A copy starts at the beginning of its workflow.
- Months keep their day: a repeat starting on the 31st gives 28 February, then 31 March.
- If the server was switched off for a while, the missed copies are made when it comes back (a few at a time).

## Looking after your repeats

Open **My work, Auto Repeat** in the sidebar, or the Repeat dialog of a document:

- **Make a copy now** (play icon): makes the next copy straight away. That date is then used up, and the schedule carries on after it.
- **Pause / Resume**: stops copies without losing the setup.
- **Stop repeating** (bin): deletes the repeat. Copies already made stay.
- If the last copy could not be made, the reason is shown in red ("Last problem"), for example a required field that is empty. The schedule carries on, so fix the original and the next copy will work. If your account is disabled, or you can no longer create these documents, the repeat is **paused** until someone looks at it.

Only you (and System Managers) can see and change your repeats. A repeat finishes by itself after its last date.
