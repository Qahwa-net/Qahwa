# Import, share, print and email rights

In the DocType designer (and the Role Permission Manager), each permission rule has four more checkboxes next to Export and Report: **Import**, **Share**, **Print** and **Email**. They are ticked by default. Untick one to stop a role from importing a file, sharing a document, printing it or sending an email about it, even though it can read or edit the document.

If a person has several roles, they keep a right when any of their roles grants it. The buttons are hidden for people who lack the right, and the server refuses the action as well.
