# Contacts and addresses

Open **Contact** or **Address** (Contacts module) to keep the people and places your business deals with.

- A **Contact** has a name, email, phone numbers, company and designation. The full name is made for you.
- An **Address** has a type (billing, shipping …), address lines, city, country and phone. Mark the preferred billing and shipping addresses.
- In the **Linked to** table, choose a DocType (for example Customer) and then the document. One contact or address can be linked to several documents, and one document can have many contacts and addresses. The document you choose must exist.

By default only System Managers can use these lists, because they hold personal details; ask your administrator to give other roles access.
