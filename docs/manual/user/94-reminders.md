# Reminders: be told about a document later

Some documents need you to come back to them: the contract to renew, the quote to chase on Friday. A **reminder** puts a notification in your bell at the time you choose.

## Set a reminder
1. Open the document and click the **bell** (Remind me).
2. Pick a time with one of the quick choices (In an hour, This evening, Tomorrow morning, Next Monday), or type your own date and time.
3. Add a note if you like (up to 500 characters) and click **Remind me**.

The dialog lists your reminders for that document. A reminder that is still waiting has a bin button to cancel it.

## What happens at the time
The notification appears in your bell (and straight away if you have Qahwa open), headed **Reminder** with your note, or with the document's name when you gave none. Opening it takes you to the document. A reminder is sent once.

## Good to know
- The time is your own clock's time; it can be at most a year ahead, and must be in the future.
- You can have 100 reminders waiting at once.
- Reminders are private: nobody else can see, change or cancel yours.
- If you can no longer read the document when the time comes (it was deleted, or your access was taken away), nothing is sent, so a reminder never gives away a document.
- Reminders are sent by the scheduler, which checks every few minutes (five by default), so a reminder can arrive a few minutes after its time.
