# Customize your Desk

Select **Customize Desk** at the top of the sidebar.

- **Sidebar & favorites:** show or hide menu items; pin documents, pages, reports, dashboards or your saved views to Favorites. Drag rows or use the up/down buttons to reorder. Favorites have their own ordering controls. Search helps find an item; clear search before reordering.
- **Groups:** reorder sidebar sections and choose which start collapsed. Clicking a section heading in the normal sidebar also saves its collapse state.
- **Home page:** choose your default workspace and add or arrange personal shortcut cards.

Choose **Save my Desk** to keep the changes across sessions and devices. **Cancel** discards the preview. **Reset to default** removes your personal choices and uses your role's defaults, or the standard Desk if no role defaults exist. Hiding a link does not change your permissions. Customize Desk itself remains available even when you hide every other link.

System Managers and Administrators can choose **Role default: ...** in the same editor and save defaults for a role. These apply to users who have no personal layout. If a user has several configured roles, the first role alphabetically is used. Role defaults can be removed from the editor.

Only links and workspaces you may access are available. Permissions are checked on the server when settings are saved and loaded. Links disappear from your effective layout when access is removed. Changes made in another browser tab can cause a save conflict; reopen the editor to load the latest settings.

Shared workspace headings, sections and dashboard tiles remain managed in **Tools → Workspaces** and **Dashboards**. This first version personalizes navigation, shortcut cards and the default workspace; it does not create a private copy of shared workspace content.

## Folding, workspaces and modules in the sidebar
- **Every group folds.** Click its title to fold or unfold it; the number is how many links it holds. **Expand all** and **Collapse all**, under *Customize Desk*, do every group at once. *Tools*, *Core* (the system's own DocTypes) and long groups start folded; the group holding the page you are on is always open. What you fold is saved with your desk, so it follows you to other devices.
- **Default order:** *My work*, your *Workspaces*, the business modules, then *Tools* and *Core*. Arrange them yourself in *Customize Desk*.
- **Workspaces** that were made for you are listed under *Workspaces*; click one to open its page.
- **Modules** have a page of their own: click the small map icon beside a module's title to see its description and all its DocTypes as cards. The home page shows one card per module.
- On a document, **assign** and **share** are the cards beside it (use the plus buttons there); the top bar no longer repeats them.
- **Drag to arrange:** drag a group's title above another group, or a link above another link in the same group, and the order is saved. *Customize Desk* has buttons for the same, which also work from the keyboard and on a phone.
- The entries under **My work** have small icons, and a workspace shows the icon its maker chose, so you can find them without reading every name.
